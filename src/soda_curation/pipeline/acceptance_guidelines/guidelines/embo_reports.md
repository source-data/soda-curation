# EMBO Reports

Apply `common.md` in full. This journal has no additional checklist items and no overrides.

Do not apply Life Science Alliance rules. Check C8 (The Paper Explained) is `N/A`: not a requirement of EMBO Reports.

## Profile only (not a checklist failure in v1.1)

For a Report, the journal pages say Results and Discussion are combined, the text is at most 25,000 characters (excluding spaces, Methods, legends and references), and there are at most 5 figures or tables. Record `character_count` and `results_discussion_combined` in the report header. Do not raise an `ACTION` for these limits until that open point in the common guideline is decided.
