## C1 · Sections present and in correct order

Same required order as the common guideline, with one addition: **The Paper Explained** sits between the Abstract and the Introduction.

Title page → Abstract (+ Keywords) → The Paper Explained → Introduction → Results → Discussion → Methods → Data Availability → Acknowledgements → Disclosure and Competing Interests Statement → References → Figure Legends → (Tables) → Expanded View Figure Legends.

## C8 · The Paper Explained

- **Applies:** EMBO Molecular Medicine only · **Level:** Required
- **Pass:** A section "The Paper Explained" is present in the manuscript between the Abstract and the Introduction.
- **Fails when:** The section is missing or misplaced.
- **Wording:** "Please add 'The Paper Explained' section between the Abstract and the Introduction."

Include C8 in Part 2. It is required for this journal.
